# Sakuga VFX

Vendored from [finktheartist/sakuga-vfx](https://github.com/finktheartist/sakuga-vfx/tree/c3dabb173928a3ad2372183103caf2e6f50953d0) under the MIT license.

The controls import a single copy of the upstream canvas and spring runtime. The button and input reactions are attached to native controls to preserve form submission, validation, focus, and React Hook Form refs. The app adapts the other elements for compact layouts, reduced motion, and stacked notifications.
